## **How do I use a condom correctly?**

1. Check the expiry date on the packaging before use.

2. Open the packaging carefully \- do not use teeth or sharp objects.

3. Make sure the condom is the right way up before applying.

4. Pinch the tip of the condom to remove air and place it on the head of an erect penis.

5. Roll the condom all the way down to the base.

6. After use, hold the base of the condom while withdrawing, wrap it in tissue and dispose of it in the bin \- never flush condoms down the toilet.

7. Use a new condom for every act of sex.

## **How should I store Bear Condoms?**

Store Bear Condoms in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and sharp objects. Avoid storing them in wallets or back pockets for extended periods, as heat and friction can damage the latex.
